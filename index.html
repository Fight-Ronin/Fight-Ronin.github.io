01 / Operational logicResearch

Research · Brown University · Current

Intrinsic Operational Logic

A portable, asset-level representation for articulated objects that encodes local rules such as latches, buttons, pedals, timers, interlocks, and coupled joints.

Implementation notes

The system mediates simulator-proposed joint updates and supports LLM-assisted authoring, controller synthesis, and semantic-trace repair for evaluating embodied interaction tasks.
